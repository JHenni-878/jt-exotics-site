You put money down, then a fixed monthly, and you drive it. No credit or income check — your down payment is your approval. Buyout option at the end on most vehicles. Insured enclosed transport available for out-of-state.

Who we are

JT Exotics is an independent vehicle-leasing intermediary. We do not own the vehicles listed here — each is offered by its respective private owner or fleet partner within our network, which spans Texas, Arizona, Florida, California and beyond. We handle the introduction, coordinate terms, and manage the process from inquiry through delivery. All lease agreements are executed directly between the vehicle's owner and the client.
